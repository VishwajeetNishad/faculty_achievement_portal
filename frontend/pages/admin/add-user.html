1. Who they are
The employee ID and email must be unique across the whole portal — they are how a person signs in.
Can also be used to sign in, instead of the email.
2. Their role
The role sets what this account can do by default. Extra abilities can be added later from User Permissions without changing the role.
This creates a full administrator. An administrator can see and change everything in the portal, including other administrators. Only create one when it is genuinely needed.
3. Department & job title
Every account belongs to exactly one department. For a Head of Department, this is also the department they are allowed to verify.
4. Sign-in details
Set the first password. The portal stores only a one-way BCrypt hash — nobody, including you, can read it back afterwards.
Use at least 8 characters. Mixing letters, numbers and a symbol makes it much harder to guess.
Tell the new user their password through a private channel and ask them to change it. It is never shown on this screen again, and it never appears in the audit trail.
5. Account status
A deactivated or suspended account cannot sign in. Nothing they submitted is deleted.
Recorded in the audit trail so the change can be explained later.
