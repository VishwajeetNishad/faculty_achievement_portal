Accreditation Report

One button instead of a week of retyping. This is every verified achievement on record, grouped the way an accreditation file needs it: department by department, year by year, split by the categories NAAC and NBA ask about. Choose a year range, then either print it straight to PDF or download the whole thing as a spreadsheet. Records still awaiting departmental verification are counted separately and never mixed into the totals.
