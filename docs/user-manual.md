# User Manual & Operating Guide

**Institution**: Noida Institute of Engineering and Technology (NIET)  
**Portal**: Faculty Achievement Portal  

---

## 1. Faculty Member Guide

### 1.1 Logging In
1. Navigate to the login page (`login.html`).
2. Enter your institutional email address (e.g., `faculty2@niet.ac.in`) or Employee ID.
3. Enter your password and click **Sign In**.
4. Upon successful login, you will be redirected to your **Faculty Dashboard**.

### 1.2 Submitting an Achievement
1. Click **+ Add Achievement** in the navigation bar.
2. Select the Achievement Category (Journal Publication, Patent, Research Grant, Workshop/FDP, or Award).
3. Enter Title, Description, Achievement Date, and Academic Year (e.g. `2024-2025`).
4. Fill in specialized category details (e.g. DOI/Impact Factor for publications, Funding Agency for grants).
5. Click **Submit Achievement**. The record will be saved in `PENDING` status.

### 1.3 Uploading a Proof Document
1. In your achievements list (`achievements.html`), locate the pending achievement.
2. Click **Upload Proof PDF**.
3. Choose a valid PDF certificate file (max 10 MB).
4. Click **Upload**. The certificate will be attached securely.

### 1.4 Exporting & Searching Records
1. Use the search bar to filter by keyword, status (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`), academic year, or category.
2. Click **Export CSV** to download a CSV file of your matching achievement records.

---

## 2. HOD (Head of Department) Guide

### 2.1 Department Dashboard & Review
1. Log in with HOD credentials (`hod.cse@niet.ac.in`).
2. View real-time department statistics and pending verification counts.
3. Open the **Verification Center** or Notification panel to see pending review requests.
4. Click **Review** on an achievement to inspect full details and download the attached PDF certificate.
5. Click **Approve** to officially verify the record, or **Reject** with mandatory feedback explaining required corrections.

---

## 3. Administrator Guide

### 3.1 Institutional Oversight
1. Log in with Admin credentials (`admin@faculty.edu`).
2. Access the **Admin Control Center** (`admin/dashboard.html`) to view campus-wide analytics and department comparison metrics.
3. Navigate to **Faculty Roster** (`admin/faculty.html`) to view active faculty members by department.
4. Access **Institutional Audit Trail** (`admin/audit-logs.html`) to inspect system transaction logs, IP addresses, and user activity.
